# Constitutional Specification — Executive Timeline Engine (ETE)

## Purpose

The Executive Timeline Engine (ETE) exists to analyze historical/longitudinal runtime outputs to establish a deterministic trajectory classification, acceleration state, confidence index, inflection points, and explainable timeline events.

* The ETE is a **longitudinal governance layer**.
* It is **not** a generic narrative generator.
* It is **not** an overrides engine.
* It is **not** a mathematical calculator of raw financial statements.

All temporal conclusions must be derived deterministically from validated historical runtime outputs.

---

## Constitutional Principles

### Principle 1 — Longitudinal Determination
All trajectory classifications, inflection points, and acceleration states must be determined strictly based on historical runtime cycles. The engine cannot assume or extrapolate data without verifiable historical inputs.

### Principle 2 — Determinism and AI Prohibition
No generative artificial governance (GenAI) may be used to classify trajectories or identify inflection points. Every state transition and event detection must follow deterministic rule paths to preserve repeatability and auditability.

### Principle 3 — Fail-Closed and Evidence Integrity
If historical runtime data is corrupt, incomplete, or contains fewer than three (3) active cycles, the ETE must immediately trigger a fail-closed response, degrading timeline confidence and reporting `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` or `FAIL_CLOSED`.

### Principle 4 — Lineage Backing
Every temporal assertion (trajectory, acceleration, detected events) must be traceable back to specific hashes of past runtime cycles. Every timeline output must contain a unified lineage hash representing the historical sequence.

### Principle 5 — Operational and Strategic Alignment
The ETE must evaluate if operational metrics (from past operational governance outputs) and strategic posture (from strategic governance outputs) align or diverge over time, triggering drift alerts when divergence occurs.
