## Phase -1: Executive Mission Alignment™

### 1. The Platform delivers Executive Advisory™?
**Analysis:** The platform structurally delivers Executive Advisory through components like `ExecutiveNarrative`, `ExecutiveRecommendation`, and `ExecutiveDecisionMemo`. However, in some localized modules (e.g., legacy Operational pages), it degrades into a "BI Dashboard" by displaying raw charts without `ExpectedKPIShift` or `DecisionIntegrityIndex`.
**Verdict:** Partial Alignment. The capability exists and is used in Governance, but is not yet fully ubiquitous.
