# HG-002B — Executive Blueprint Compliance (EBC)

## Executive Blueprint Constitution

### Regra Institucional
A BP (Balance Sheet) não deve ser copiada literalmente; ela deve servir como **referência arquitetural**.
Isso evita que todas as páginas fiquem visualmente iguais. O objetivo é que compartilhem a mesma lógica de decisão, hierarquia de informação e qualidade da experiência executiva, preservando as necessidades específicas de cada domínio (simulação, governança, DRE, DFC, cadastro, workflows etc.).

### Mission
The Balance Sheet (BP) page is the official Executive Blueprint of the Illumine Governance Platform.
From this point forward, every page created or refactored MUST converge toward the BP executive experience.
The objective is NOT visual similarity.
The objective is cognitive consistency.
Every page must guide an executive through the same quality of decision-making experience while respecting its own business purpose.

---

## Golden Rule
Never begin implementation.
Never replace components.
Never modify layout.
First redesign the Executive Architecture.
Only after explicit approval may implementation begin.
