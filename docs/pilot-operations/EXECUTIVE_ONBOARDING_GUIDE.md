# Executive Onboarding Guide (EXECUTIVE_ONBOARDING_GUIDE)

Welcome to the Illumine Platform onboarding flow. This guide helps pilot C-Levels and Board Members master the system's fiduciaries and simulation boundaries.

## Onboarding Checklist Journey

Executives must complete these 7 steps in order to unlock full production validation:

1. **System Activation**: Complete environment boot and verify cryptographic keys.
2. **Workspace Configuration**: Map active entities, subsidiaries, and group relationships.
3. **Causality Understanding**: Review and validate the causal nexuses mapping transactions to governance risk.
4. **Command Center Verification**: Navigate the core runtime observability logs and check system status.
5. **Simulation Boundaries**: Confirm understanding that the system uses zero generative AI or speculative forecasting.
6. **Feedback Submission**: Submit at least one friction point or usability review in the feedback module.
7. **Board Approval**: Execute the digital signature to declare readiness for production go-live.

## Safeguards & ordered completion

To maintain audit integrity, shortcuts are disabled. Attempting to skip steps will trigger a `VIOLATION_ONBOARDING_ORDER` audit log.
