# Executive Revenue Office™ Registry

## Purpose
To manage the complete lifecycle of revenue operations for the Illumine platform, transforming leads into recurring revenue at a global scale.

## Responsibilities
- Manage the acquisition pipeline for the platform.
- Structure, version, and negotiate Executive Proposals.
- Manage Platform Commercial Plans, Pricing, and Packaging.
- Orchestrate Subscriptions, Entitlements, and Licenses.
- Control Billing, Invoicing, and Payments.
- Track Partner Commissions and Customer Success metrics (MRR, ARR, Churn).

## Boundaries
- Operates exclusively at the **Platform Tenant** level.
- Does not interact with or store data related to the operational sales of Customer Tenants.
- Connects to the Customer Tenant strictly via the issuance and revocation of Licenses/Entitlements.

## Dependencies
- Multi-Tenant Architecture (to issue licenses).
- Capability Registry (to map entitlements to features).
- Authentication Layer (to enforce licensing).
