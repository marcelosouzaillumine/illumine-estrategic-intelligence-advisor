# UX Architecture & Journeys

## 1. Sales Team Journey (Revenue Manager)
- **Dashboard**: Pipeline health, MRR forecast, Win-rate.
- **Leads & Accounts**: Prospecting, qualification, logging activities.
- **Proposal Workspace**: Constructing premium proposals, adjusting pricing sensitivity, generating the secure link for the client.
- **Contracts & Billing**: Monitoring invoice payments and active subscriptions.

## 2. Advisor Journey (Partner)
- **Portfolio View**: Managing their own book of business.
- **Client Proposals**: Generating proposals specifically for their acquired clients (prices dictated by Platform Pricing Engine, potentially with approved discounts).
- **Commissions**: Viewing earned commissions and payouts.

## 3. Customer Admin Journey (Client)
- **Proposal Acceptance**: Accessing the premium `Executive Proposal Workspace™` link. Reviewing Diagnóstico, Solução, Investimento, and performing the Aceite Digital.
- **Subscription Management**: Viewing active plan, billing history, and upcoming renewals.
- **Licensing**: Assigning purchased licenses to their internal users based on granted entitlements.
