# Executive Revenue Constitution

## 1. Domain Separation Principle
Revenue belongs strictly to the Platform Domain (Illumine). Customer operational revenue belongs strictly to the Tenant Domain (Commercial Governance Office). They must never intersect.

## 2. Authoritative State Principle
The Billing state is authoritative for all financial obligations. No service is provisioned without a confirmed billing state, except in explicit trial or grant conditions.

## 3. Entitlement-Driven Access
Access to capabilities is not granted by payment directly, nor by subscription directly. Access is granted solely by **Entitlements**. A Subscription grants an Entitlement; an Entitlement generates a License; a License resolves to User Permissions.

## 4. Platform Pricing Authority
Pricing, packaging, and commercial plans are governed by the Platform Pricing Engine. Tenants cannot alter platform pricing structures.

## 5. Global Readiness Principle
Every commercial model must be globally aware. Currencies, timezones, languages, and local taxes must be parameterized at the foundation.

## 6. Commercial Auditability
Every commercial action (creation, modification, cancellation, upgrade) must generate an immutable audit trail.
