# ADR-035 — Executive Advisory Lifecycle Architecture

> **Status**: Approved  
> **Date**: 2026-07-29  
> **Context**: Wave 15D Operational Readiness & Advisory Governance Gate v1.0  
> **Scope**: Advisory Case Lifecycle & Workflow Engine Architecture

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

As recomendações executivas emitidas para clientes corporativos devem ser geridas como casos de aconselhamento (`AdvisoryCase`) acompanhados ao longo de todo o ciclo operacional.

---

## 2. Decision: The AdvisoryCase Workflow Architecture

Fica estabelecido pelo Architecture Review Board (ARB) que todo processo de aconselhamento rodará no pacote `@illumine/advisory-workflow-engine` sob o fluxo de estados:

$$\text{OPEN} \rightarrow \text{ANALYZING} \rightarrow \text{RECOMMENDATION\_READY} \rightarrow \text{EXECUTIVE\_REVIEW} \rightarrow \text{APPROVED} \rightarrow \text{IMPLEMENTING} \rightarrow \text{MEASURED} \rightarrow \text{LEARNED}$$

---

## 3. Consequences

- Rastreabilidade de ciclo de vida completo do atendimento de consultoria cognitiva.
