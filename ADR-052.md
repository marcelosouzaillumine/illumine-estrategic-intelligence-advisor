# ADR-052 — Executive Question Generation Framework

> **Status**: Approved  
> **Date**: 2026-07-29  
> **Context**: Wave 17 Executive Operating Experience & Real-World Intelligence Activation v1.0  
> **Scope**: Recommended Questions & Proactive Prompts Framework

---

## 1. Context & Problem Statement

Antecipar os questionamentos do executivo sugerindo proativamente as melhores perguntas investigativas diante de desvios operacionais ou financeiros.

---

## 2. Decision: The ExecutiveQuestionEngine

Fica estabelecido pelo Architecture Review Board (ARB) que o pacote `@illumine/executive-question-engine` gerará perguntas recomendadas proativas categorizadas em Diagnóstica, Estratégica, Operacional e de Impacto.

---

## 3. Consequences

- Aceleração do processo de tomada de decisão com prompts investigativos prontos.
